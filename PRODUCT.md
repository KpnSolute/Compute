# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cafeteria managers, assistant managers, and staff at Miami Job Corps Center (a federal vocational training program). They use this tool daily — logging inventory receipts, recording pulls, reviewing compliance, and generating end-of-month reports. Most interactions happen at a desktop workstation in the cafeteria office. The primary user (manager-level) needs confident, fast access to numbers; staff-level users do data entry.

## Product Purpose

MJCC Management Portal is the operational backbone of the MJCC cafeteria: inventory tracking across weekly receiving and issuing cycles, monthly roll-up reporting, HACCP compliance logging, event scheduling, and menu management. Success means the manager can close a month accurately in under an hour — with correct balances, printed reports, and zero reconciliation surprises.

## Brand Personality

Professional, modern, trusted. The system should feel like a step above a generic government tool — polished dark-navy design system that respects the institutional context without feeling bureaucratic. The UI earns confidence by being precise and reliable.

## Anti-references

- Generic government USDA/FSIS spreadsheet aesthetic (flat tables, no hierarchy)
- Overly casual SaaS (pastel colors, bubbly shapes, marketing-speak copy)
- Heavy enterprise UI (cluttered toolbars, too many clicks, modal-on-modal)

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first.** Every screen leads with the data the manager needs. UI chrome serves numbers, not the other way around.
2. **Institutional precision.** Navy + white + green — the palette reflects the program's identity, not trends. Consistency over novelty.
3. **Zero surprise commits.** Any action that writes to the database (stage, commit, publish) shows a clear confirmation step with what will change.
4. **One role, one view.** The portal surfaces different capabilities by role level (staff / assistant / manager / admin) — each person sees only what they act on.
5. **Printable at all times.** Reports must look as good on paper as on screen. Print and CSV are first-class, not afterthoughts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High-contrast navy/white pairs at well above 4.5:1. Touch targets ≥ 44px for tablet use. No motion required for any functional flow (animations are additive only).
